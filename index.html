02 — About me

Part designer, part storyteller, full-time aesthetic enthusiast.

I'm Salwa — a graphic designer from Tripoli, Lebanon, who studied Graphic Design at the Arab Open University and fell in love with the place where design meets people: social media.

These days I plan content calendars, style and shoot products, edit reels, write the captions and answer the DMs — so every post looks lovely and actually moves the needle. I've helped gemstone and jewelry brands, gyms, cafés, juice bars and tech startups find their visual voice, in Lebanon and across the Gulf.
