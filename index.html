03 — About me

Design that looks beautiful and does its job.

I'm Salwa, a graphic designer from Tripoli, Lebanon. I studied Graphic Design at the Arab Open University, and I work where brand design meets social media.

I build identities and guidelines, design magazines, brochures and packaging, and run social content end to end. That means planning calendars, styling and shooting products, editing reels, writing captions and talking with customers, so every piece looks considered and actually performs.

My clients include gemstone and jewellery brands, cafés, juice bars, gyms and tech startups, in Lebanon and across the Gulf.
